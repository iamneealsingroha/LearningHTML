Save Water

We must save water because only a tiny fraction of Earth's water is usable freshwater, and it is essential for the survival of all humans, animals, and plants. As populations grow and climate change causes severe shortages, wasting this vital resource threatens food security, harms natural ecosystems, and leads to dangerous water crises. Conserving water through simple daily habits ensures that current and future generations have enough clean water to live.

  • Freshwater is highly limited: The Earth is covered in water, but only 2.5% of it is fresh and drinkable. The rest is saltwater or locked in glaciers.
  • Reduces energy consumption: Pumping, treating, and heating water requires massive amounts of electricity. Saving water directly cuts down on energy use and carbon emissions.
  • Ensures food security: Agriculture relies on water, consuming over 70% of global freshwater supplies. Conserving water ensures there is enough to grow food for a growing population.
