Samosa

Ingredients

For the Dough

  • All-purpose flour (maida): 2 cups
  • Carom seeds (ajwain): 1 tsp
  • Salt: 1 tsp
  • Ghee or oil: 1/4 cup (melted)
  • Water: 1/3 cup (or as needed to make a stiff dough)

For the Potato Filling

  • Boiled potatoes: 3-4 medium, peeled and coarsely crumbled
  • Green peas: 1/2 cup (optional)
  • Cumin seeds: 1/2 tsp
  • Ginger-garlic paste or minced ginger: 1 tsp
  • Green chilies: 1-2, finely chopped
  • Red chili powder: 1/2 tsp
  • Cumin powder & coriander powder: 1/2 tsp each
  • Chaat masala or amchur (dry mango powder): 1 tsp
  • Fennel powder: 1/2 tsp
  • Fresh coriander leaves: 1 Tbsp, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp (for cooking filling) + plenty for deep frying

Steps

  1. Make the Dough

    1. Mix the flour, carom seeds, and salt in a large bowl.
    2. Add the melted ghee or oil. Rub it into the flour with your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs and holds its shape when squeezed.
    3. Slowly add water a little at a time to knead a very firm, stiff dough.
    4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 20 to 30 minutes

  2. Prepare the Filling

    1. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
    2. Add the cumin seeds and let them crackle.
    3. Add the chopped green chilies, ginger, and green peas, sautéing for a minute.
    4. Stir in the red chili powder, cumin powder, coriander powder, fennel powder, and chaat masala.
    5. Add the crumbled boiled potatoes and salt. Mix well and cook for 3 to 4 minutes so the spices coat the potatoes.
    6. Turn off the heat, stir in the fresh coriander, and let the mixture cool completely.

  3. Shape the Samosas

    1. Divide the rested dough into equal small balls.
    2. Roll out one ball into a thin oval shape (about 1 mm thick).
    3. Cut the oval cleanly across the middle ito create two equal semi-circles.
    4. Dip your finger in water and run it along the straight cut edge of the semi-circle.
    5. Fold the two straight ends together to form a cone, pressing the seam firmly to seal it.
    6. Stuff the cone with 1.5 to 2 tablespoons of the cooled potato filling.
    7. Dab a little more water along the top inside edges, pinch, and fold to seal the top completely closed. Repeat with the remaining dough.

  4. Fry the Samosas

    1. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over low-to-medium heat. The oil should be only gently hot (around 300°F / 150°C)—not boiling hot—so the crust stays crisp and doesn't bubble.
    2. Carefully slide in a few samosas at a time.
    3. Fry slowly on medium-low heat for 10 to 15 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and crispy all over.
    4. . Serve hot with mint-coriander chutney and tamarind chutney.
